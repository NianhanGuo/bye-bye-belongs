Inspired by Kafka’s story of a lost doll

FROM: A LITTLE RED SCARFsomewhere with a breeze

My dear you,

Please don’t picture me alone on that train. Picture me at the window, watching the world become somewhere neither of us has been.

I have spent so long keeping you warm. Perhaps, just for a little while, I wanted to find out what the wind felt like.

I haven’t forgotten the shape of your shoulders. Some things are a home you carry, even when you wander.

With all my loose threads,
Your little red scarf

P.S. You can be sad and still wish me a good journey.
